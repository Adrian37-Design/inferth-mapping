Cookie Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Covers inferthmapping.co.zw, the tracking console and the login pages.

In short: there are no advertising, profiling or third-party analytics cookies on this site. The only storage we set is what is needed to sign you in and to remember that you have seen the cookie notice.

1. What we store, and why

NameTypePurposeDuration
token (console session) Local storage Keeps you signed in to the tracking console and authorises your API requests. Without it the console cannot work. Until you sign out or the session expires
inferth_cookie_consent_v1 Local storage Records that you have seen the cookie notice and when, so it is not shown on every page. Until you clear site data
Console interface preferences Local storage Remembers the map layer, panel state and similar display choices on your device. Until you clear site data

These items are strictly necessary or are set at your request, so they do not require consent under commonly applied cookie rules. We still show a notice so you know what is stored.

2. What we do not use

  • No Google Analytics, Meta pixel, Hotjar or similar tracking scripts.
  • No advertising, retargeting or cross-site profiling cookies.
  • No session-replay or heat-mapping tools.
  • No sale or sharing of cookie data with data brokers.

3. Third-party resources loaded by our pages

Some pages load libraries and map tiles from public content-delivery networks so the console and website work. These requests necessarily disclose your IP address and browser headers to that provider, but they do not set advertising cookies of ours:

4. Managing cookies

  • Use cookie settings to review the notice again.
  • Your browser lets you block or delete cookies and site data. Blocking the session item signs you out of the console.
  • Clearing site data removes the consent record, so the notice appears again on your next visit.

5. Changes

We update this page whenever the storage we use changes. The effective date at the top shows the current version.
