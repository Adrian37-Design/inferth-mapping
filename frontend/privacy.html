Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Applies to inferthmapping.co.zw, the Inferth Mapping web console and the tracking platform behind it.

In short: we process the account details you give us and the telemetry your trackers send so the tracking service works, we do not sell personal information, we run no advertising or analytics trackers, and you can ask for a copy or the deletion of your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Inferth Mapping is a telematics and fleet-management platform operated by Inferth Projects, Harare, Zimbabwe (“Inferth Mapping”, “we”, “us”). We are the controller of the account, billing and website information described here.

For vehicle telemetry we are usually a processor acting on the instructions of the business account that owns the tracker (the “fleet account”). If your employer or a transport operator tracks a vehicle you drive, that organisation decides why and how the data is used, and you should contact them first. We will help them answer your request.

Where a reseller supplies your tracking account, the reseller is your first point of contact for service and billing questions, and both the reseller and Inferth Mapping process the data needed to run that account.

3. Location data and the people who drive tracked vehicles

A vehicle tracker reports where that vehicle is. That can reveal the movements of the person driving it. Fleet accounts must have a lawful basis for tracking their vehicles and must inform drivers and staff that tracking is in use, in line with Zimbabwean employment and data-protection requirements.

We do not use vehicle location data for advertising, profiling or resale, and we do not disclose it to anyone except the fleet account it belongs to, a reseller that operates that account, our infrastructure providers, or where the law requires it.

4. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract): create and secure your account, receive and display telemetry, send alerts you configured, issue invoices and receipts, provide support.
  • To keep the platform safe and working (legitimate interests): detect abuse, prevent fraud, debug faults, enforce fair use, keep audit logs, protect the service from attacks.
  • To meet legal obligations: tax, accounting and record-keeping, and responding to valid legal requests.
  • With your consent, where we ask for it — for example if you opt in to product updates. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Who we share it with

  • Your reseller, if your account is supplied through one, so they can support you and bill you.
  • Payment providers (for example Paynow and the mobile-money networks behind it) to process a payment you start. They receive the amount, a reference and your contact detail — not your telemetry.
  • Messaging and email providers used to deliver alerts, invitations and receipts (for example a WhatsApp gateway and an email delivery service).
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that run the servers and database on our instructions.
  • Authorities where we are legally required to respond, or to protect someone’s safety.
  • A successor, if the business is sold or reorganised, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Position history follows the retention of your plan — currently 30 days on entry plans and 90 days on higher plans, unless a longer period is agreed in writing. The newest fixes for each device are kept so the last known position still works.
  • Alerts and insights are kept while they are useful for the fleet account, and are deleted with the account.
  • Billing and tax records are kept for the period Zimbabwean law requires after the end of the tax year, even after an account is closed.
  • Account and audit records are kept while the account is active and for a short period after closure to handle disputes, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Server logs are kept for a short operational window and then discarded.

7. How we protect it

Access is role-based and scoped to a single company account. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, sessions expire, and payment-gateway credentials are encrypted at rest. Data in transit is protected with TLS. Access to production systems is limited to people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we investigate suspected incidents and notify affected accounts where the law requires it.

8. Where your data is processed

The platform is hosted in the cloud, so data may be processed on servers outside Zimbabwe by our infrastructure and messaging providers. We choose providers that protect data to a comparable standard and keep the set of providers small.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • confirm what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information (see data deletion);
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including direct marketing;
  • provide your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw a consent you gave earlier.

Email sales@inferth.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line. We may need to verify that you are the account holder or an authorised contact before acting. We answer within 30 days, and tell you if we need longer and why.

If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the relevant Zimbabwean authority, the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ), or to your local data-protection regulator.

10. Children

The platform is a business service for people aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18. Trackers must not be used to monitor children or their belongings. If you believe a child’s information has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Email and messages

Service messages — invitations, password and sign-in codes, receipts, invoices and security notices — are part of the service and are sent to the account contact. Optional product updates are only sent if you opt in, and every such email includes an unsubscribe link. Transactional messages may still be sent after you unsubscribe.

12. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies and local storage. Details, including what is stored and for how long, are in the cookie policy.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page and its effective date, and tell account administrators about material changes by email or in the console before they take effect.
