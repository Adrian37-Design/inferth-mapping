Refund Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Applies to payments made directly to Inferth Mapping and, through us, to reseller billing.

In short: we refund duplicate or mistaken charges in full, refund an unused wallet balance on request, and consider pro-rata refunds for prepaid periods we cannot deliver. Used subscription time, hardware and third-party network costs are not refundable.

1. Scope

This policy explains how refunds work for subscriptions, the PAYD wallet, lifetime packages and Console Rental. If you bought tracking through a reseller, the reseller is the merchant: contact them first, and we will support them in resolving your request. Where Inferth Mapping is the merchant, this policy applies directly.

2. Monthly and yearly subscriptions

  • A subscription covers the period it was paid for. Time already used is not refundable.
  • If you cancel part-way through a paid period, tracking stays active until the end of that period. We do not automatically refund the remainder.
  • If we cannot provide the service for a material part of a prepaid period (for example a prolonged outage on our side), you may request a pro-rata refund or credit for the affected days.
  • Yearly plans are charged at ten months’ rate for twelve months of service. A refund of a yearly plan is calculated on the months not yet used, at the monthly rate that applies to your fleet band, less any discount already received.

3. PAYD wallet

  • Wallet top-ups are credit for future tracking, not a subscription payment.
  • Usage already charged for tracked hours is not refundable.
  • An unused wallet balance can be refunded on request, less any payment-provider fee we actually incurred and any amount owed on the account.
  • A wallet credited twice for one payment is corrected immediately — see “Duplicate and mistaken payments”.

4. Lifetime packages

  • You may cancel a Lifetime purchase within 14 days of payment for a full refund, provided the account has not tracked vehicles under that package.
  • After that, or once the package has been used, the purchase is non-refundable because it is a once-off entitlement.
  • If a Lifetime account is closed for a breach of the terms, no refund is due.

5. Console Rental

Console Rental is invoiced monthly in advance on the renter’s fleet size on the billing day. A month already started is not refundable. If you reduce your fleet mid-month, the lower tier applies from the next invoice.

6. Duplicate and mistaken payments

If you are charged twice for the same invoice, pay the wrong invoice, or a payment is confirmed by the provider but not reflected on your account, contact us with the payment reference. Once we verify it with the provider we refund the excess to the original payment method, or credit it to your account if you prefer. These corrections are made in full and are not subject to the limits in section 2.

7. What is not refundable

  • Hardware, trackers, SIM cards, installation and data bundles bought from us or a third party;
  • third-party charges such as mobile-network data or payment-provider fees;
  • optional features and add-ons once the period they cover has started;
  • accounts suspended or terminated for unlawful tracking, abuse or non-payment;
  • periods where tracking failed because of a device, SIM, wiring or installer fault outside our control.

8. How to request a refund

  1. Email sales@inferth.com with the subject “Refund request”, or message +263 78 300 9275 on WhatsApp.
  2. Include the account company, the invoice number or payment reference, the amount, the date and the reason.
  3. We acknowledge within 2 business days and aim to decide within 10 business days.
  4. Approved refunds are paid back to the original method through the provider. Depending on the provider, funds appear within 5–10 business days; mobile-money reversals can take longer.
  5. You receive a written outcome with a reference, whether or not the refund is approved.

9. Chargebacks

Please contact us before asking your bank or provider to reverse a payment — most issues are corrected the same day. If a chargeback is raised, we may pause the affected account while the provider investigates, and restore it once the matter is resolved.

10. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy reduces a right you have under Zimbabwean consumer law. Where that law gives you a stronger remedy than this policy, that remedy applies.
