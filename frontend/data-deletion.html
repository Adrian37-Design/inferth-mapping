Data Deletion Request

Ask us to delete your account information, vehicle telemetry or both.

What we delete: account users, vehicle records, position history, alerts, geofences and insights. What we must keep: invoices and payment records for the period Zimbabwean tax law requires, and a short record of the request itself.

1. Before you submit

  • If your tracking is supplied by a reseller, tell them as well — they may hold their own copy of your account and billing records.
  • Deletion is permanent. Exported reports and invoices you have downloaded remain yours.
  • If you want to keep the account but remove one vehicle, delete that device in the console instead.
  • We confirm the request with the account email before deleting, to stop someone else deleting your data.

2. Submit the request

3. What happens next

  1. You receive a reference number on screen, and a copy by email if the address matches an account.
  2. We verify the request with the account contact (usually within 2 business days).
  3. Once verified, account data is deleted within 30 days. Backups roll over within a further short period.
  4. We email the outcome, including anything we were legally required to keep.

Prefer email? Write to sales@inferth.com with “Data deletion” in the subject line, or message +263 78 300 9275 on WhatsApp.
