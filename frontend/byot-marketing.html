1. BRING YOUR OWN TRACKER (BYOT)

Already have a GPS tracker? Put it to better use.

You do not necessarily need to purchase new tracking hardware to use Inferth Mapping. Our Bring Your Own Tracker model allows you to connect compatible GPS devices to our platform and monitor them through one centralised dashboard.

Supported tracker protocols and devices

GT06-Compatible
Standard binary protocol utilized by hundreds of Chinese and global trackers (Accurate, Concox, Jimi).
Port 5023 / 5002
SinoTrack
Direct TCP binary & text protocol for ST-901, ST-902, ST-905 and related models.
Port 5013
Teltonika AVL
Enterprise Codec 8/8E decoder supporting FMC130, FMB920, and full CAN/OBD telemetry parameters.
Port 5027
H02 Protocol
Widely deployed protocol for vehicle immobilizers, mini asset trackers and OBD dongles.
Port 5013 / 5090
GPS103 / TK103
Classic ASCII text command-based tracker format compatible with Coban and clones.
Port 5001
Other Compatible Devices
Custom TCP/UDP reporting trackers configurable to point to custom domain and port.
Custom Port Ingestion

Specific tracker models must be assessed before connection. Compatibility depends on the device model, firmware, communication protocol and ability to configure its reporting server.

10. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tracker compatibility, pricing, and infrastructure.

1. Can I use my existing GPS tracker with Inferth Mapping?
Yes. If your tracker uses a supported communication protocol and allows its reporting IP and port to be configured, it can connect to our platform.
2. What tracker protocols are supported?
Inferth Mapping currently supports GT06-compatible devices, SinoTrack, Teltonika AVL, H02, GPS103 and several others. Submit your tracker details for assessment.
3. How does progressive volume pricing work?
Each vehicle rate applies only to vehicles within that specific band, rather than the entire fleet. For example, on the Basic plan, the first 5 vehicles are $15 each, and vehicles 6–20 are $12 each.
4. What is the difference between Basic and Premium?
Basic covers essential GPS tracking, 30-day route history, basic alerts and fleet reports. Premium adds CAN/OBD telemetry (where supported), fuel monitoring, automated WhatsApp reports, priority alerts and 90-day history.
5. What is Pay As You Drive (PAYD)?
PAYD is a daily billing option ($0.40/vehicle/day) for vehicles that only need tracking occasionally. Optional features like route history or diagnostics can be activated for fixed fees per period.
6. Can Inferth Mapping immobilise a vehicle remotely?
Yes, where the installed hardware and vehicle electrical configuration support relay control, and the feature has been verified, tested and safely enabled with appropriate permissions.
7. What is Console Rental?
Console Rental is a reseller tier for tracking businesses that want to provide fleet management to their own clients using Inferth Mapping's software infrastructure.
8. Does Inferth Mapping provide SIM cards and installation?
Customers typically provide their own SIM cards and arrange physical installation. Inferth Mapping can recommend or quote installation and hardware separately if required.
