  How it works

Connected in three steps

1

Send your model and IMEI

WhatsApp us the tracker model and its 15-digit IMEI. We confirm the protocol and prepare the exact command.

2

Point the tracker at Inferth

We send the server address, port and SMS syntax for your brand. You apply it once, from the tracker's own SIM.

3

Watch it go live

The vehicle appears on the map as soon as the first packet arrives, usually within two minutes of a GPS fix.
