What is a Once-Off Tracking Payment?

Instead of paying every month, you pay one amount now and your vehicle stays tracked for the period you choose — with zero monthly charges. No debit orders. No renewal reminders. No surprises on your phone.

Starter Pass — 2 Years Pay $89 today. Your vehicle tracks for the next 24 months. After 2 years, top up again or switch to monthly.
Pro Pass — Lifetime Pay $149 once. Your vehicle tracks forever — for as long as Inferth Mapping operates. No renewals. No expiry. We honour this commitment.
Fleet Bundle — 5+ Vehicles Tracking 5 or more trucks? We negotiate a bulk once-off rate per vehicle. Contact us for a custom quote.

The Lifetime Pass, explained in full

One payment per vehicle, made once. No renewal date, no debit order, no top-up prompt. Below is exactly what that money buys — and what it does not.

Covered by your once-off payment
  • Platform access for that vehicle, with no end date — live map, trip history, geofences, alerts and the full Pro feature set, for as long as Inferth Mapping is running.
  • Every future update — new features, reports and fixes land on your account automatically, at no extra cost.
  • Hosting, database storage and backups — the servers behind the map are our cost, not a line on your bill.
  • WhatsApp and SMS alert delivery for geofence, power-cut and theft alerts, within fair-use limits.
  • Transfer with the vehicle — the pass lives on the tracker’s IMEI, so a buyer inherits it.
Paid separately — not in the pass
  • The tracker unit — use your own or we quote and fit one for you. Hardware is never bundled into the pass price.
  • Installation and wiring — an auto-electrician fee quoted per vehicle. Remote engine cut-off only works once the relay is physically fitted.
  • SIM airtime and data — roughly $1–$3/month paid straight to Econet or NetOne. It is the tracker’s phone bill, not our subscription.
  • Replacement trackers — a new unit after theft, damage or write-off is a hardware purchase.
  • Custom integrations, on-site training or consulting — quoted separately for Fleet and Enterprise work.
How “lifetime” is measured: it means the operating life of the Inferth Mapping platform for one vehicle — not the life of the truck, and not a fixed number of years. The pass does not tick down while a vehicle is parked, offline or in the workshop. It simply never comes up for renewal again.
If I pay $149 once, what exactly do I never pay again?

The monthly per-vehicle platform fee. A Pro Pass vehicle is never billed a subscription, a renewal, an annual licence or a reactivation fee. Monthly plans run $12–$17 per vehicle, so the pass has paid for itself in under 13 months — everything after that is free.

Is there a catch, a cap or a hidden expiry?

No. The pass unlocks the full Professional feature set on that one vehicle permanently. The only recurring money in the whole setup is the tracker’s own SIM data, paid to your network operator, and the platform never touches it.

What if the platform itself ever shuts down?

Lifetime is bounded by the platform’s operating life, which is why we sell it once rather than as a short-term promo. We are building for the long haul and we honour every active pass. If the platform were ever wound down, you would get advance notice and a full export of your tracking history.

What happens when the 2-year Starter Pass runs out?

Nothing is auto-charged. At month 24 that vehicle simply stops reporting until you choose: buy another pass at the price then in force, or move that single vehicle onto Monthly or the Prepaid Wallet. Your history stays on the account either way.

My tracker failed or was replaced — is the pass gone?

No. The pass belongs to the vehicle, keyed to its tracker’s IMEI. Fit a replacement unit and we re-link the pass to the new IMEI at no extra charge — you pay for the hardware itself, never for the subscription twice.

Can I sell the vehicle with the pass on it?

Yes, and it is a genuine resale point. The pass follows the tracker, not the owner, so the new owner keeps whatever tracking is left — forever, on a Pro Pass. Send us their details and we move account access across.

Must the whole fleet go once-off?

No. Passes are bought per vehicle, so you can mix freely — a few trucks on Pro Pass lifetime, the rest on Monthly or Prepaid. Fleet owners buying 5 or more get a negotiated per-vehicle rate.

How do I pay, and how soon does tracking start?

Paynow checkout with EcoCash, OneMoney or Zimswitch, priced in USD. You get a WhatsApp confirmation and a receipt the moment it clears, and the vehicle is live on the platform within 2 hours of confirmation.

Can I get my money back if I change my mind?

A pass activates the moment payment is confirmed, so once a vehicle has gone live the payment is final. If you are unsure which pass fits your fleet, talk to us before paying — we will size it with you rather than sell you the wrong one.
